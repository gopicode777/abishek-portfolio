About Abishek

Where craft meets
quiet obsession.

Eight years spent chasing light — as a photographer and a graphic designer — has taught me one thing: the best work never feels designed. It feels inevitable. Click a card to read more.

01Who I Am
A visual storyteller based between studio and street — equally at home behind a medium-format camera or inside a design file, building brand identities that photograph as well as they print.
02Experience
8+ years shooting weddings, editorial, and brand campaigns across three countries, alongside 6 years designing identity systems and packaging for boutique and enterprise clients alike.
03Creative Philosophy
Restraint is a design decision. I chase the one true frame and the one right typeface rather than a hundred good-enough options — precision over volume, always.
04Education
BFA in Visual Communication, National Institute of Design — followed by an independent apprenticeship under two documentary photographers across Southeast Asia.
05Achievements
Featured in International Photography Awards 2023, two Awwwards-recognised brand launches, and 40+ published editorial covers.
