01 — About

From behind the lens to inside the interface.

AbishekUI/UX Designer · Nagapattinam, TN

I hold a B.Sc. in Visual Communication and started out behind a camera — street, event, and food photography that taught me to see composition, light, and timing before I ever opened Figma.

Now I bring that same eye to digital product design: wireframes, high-fidelity UI, and the small motion details that make a screen feel considered instead of assembled.

I work end to end — research and structure first, then visual design and prototyping that's ready to build.
