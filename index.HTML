01 — About

From viewfinder to wireframe.

I hold a B.Sc. in Visual Communication and spent years finding the story inside a frame — street scenes, weddings, product shots, editorial layouts. That training didn't stop applying once I moved to screens.

Today I design user interfaces and digital experiences with the same discipline: establish hierarchy before decoration, earn every pixel of attention, and never let the craft get in the way of the message. Photography taught me to see; design lets me build what I see.

I work across the full visual stack — wireframes, high-fidelity UI, prototyping, and the motion that makes an interface feel alive.
